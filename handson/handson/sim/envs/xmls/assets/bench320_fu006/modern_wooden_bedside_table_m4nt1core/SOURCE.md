preserved beside the Isaac Sim asset. `modern_wooden_bedside_table.usd` was
converted with Isaac Sim 5.1 `omni.kit.asset_converter` 5.0.17 using separate
meshes and PreviewSurface materials. The conversion preserves the cabinet and
the two authored drawer nodes independently.

Runtime use in FU006:

- The cabinet and upper drawer provide the static visible fixture.
- The lower downloaded drawer follows the validated PartNet prismatic link.
- Static fixture collision is authored from the downloaded cabinet meshes.
- The original PartNet articulation remains the physical success frame.
