Meshes transformed to metres, source UVs retained, selected submeshes extracted, microwave knobs articulated and door unbaked/seated, tongs split at the spring bend, source-derived convex collision generated. The source hot-dog title says CC0, but its downloaded license.txt states CC BY 4.0; attribution is retained accordingly.
