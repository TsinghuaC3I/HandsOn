Modifications for HandsOn GEN3: uniform scale to a 270 mm total closed height,
coordinate conversion, English USD mesh/link names, passive revolute joint at
the source lid pivot, separate source-derived convex collision, finite mass
properties, USD materials preserving source material factors. The lid and its
existing connector form one moving link. The existing pedal and bottom parts
are fixed with the bin body. Source animation is not played by the simulator.
No visible or functional geometry is created. The original GLB is preserved.

The fixed shell's open underside is backed by the existing separate source
bottom mesh. Its mass distribution is estimated from the source triangle
areas; no triangles are added to close the source seam.
