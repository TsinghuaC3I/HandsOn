## Modifications

Converted the original surfaces to USD; retained source materials and texture UVs. Normalized the enclosure uniformly to meters. Reused Cylinder098 for two independently jointed knobs; aligned its measured axis and adjusted radial/axial scale to a 55 mm maximum swept diameter and 45 mm height. Removed surrounding source controls within the recorded hand-clearance areas. Derived invisible convex collision from these source surfaces. No new visible geometry was modeled.
